01

Functional Specification

Every module below reflects the live platform. Items marked confirmed are live and working today; gap marks scope that isn't on the live platform yet, deferred rather than assumed; unverified marks scope inferred from platform copy rather than a live screen. Admin is out of scope here and will be added once its details are shared.

A — Customer App

Catalog & Navigation

CUST-01
confirmed
  • 19 categories total (11 top-level + 8 under "More"), each opening a mega-menu of up to 11 subcategories
  • Category listing pages: subcategory chips, price-range filter, star-rating filter, multi-select brand filter, 5-way sort (Featured / Price ↑ / Price ↓ / Rating / Newest)
  • Search with the same faceted filters — 60 results returned for a single test query
  • Flash Deals page: shared countdown timer across listed deals, per-product "% sold" progress bar

Product Detail

CUST-02
confirmed
  • Image gallery, price + discount badge, stock urgency messaging ("only 1 left")
  • Per-product seller attribution card (confirmed genuine multi-vendor — two products showed two different sellers)
  • Review infrastructure present but unseeded ("no reviews yet — be first after purchase")
  • Variant selector on apparel (size, shown as tappable M/L/XL/2XL-style options); the platform's data model supports variants generically (size, color, etc.), so the mobile selector is built generic rather than size-only — it will handle color variants the moment any seller lists one
  • No Q&A section on the product page

Wishlist

CUST-03
confirmed
  • Dual behavior: heart icon on product cards toggles anonymously (local/optimistic UI, no login prompt)
  • Full wishlist list page is login-gated (/account/wishlist)

Cart & Checkout

CUST-04
confirmed
  • Slide-out cart drawer + full cart page; VAT-inclusive pricing (5% UAE VAT); free-shipping threshold nudge
  • 3-step checkout: Shipping → Payment → Review
  • Shipping: address types (Home/Office/Other), Emirate + City cascading select — all 7 emirates present; saved addresses persist to account
  • Payment: Tap Payments integration (Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay) plus a separate Cash on Delivery option (adds AED 10 handling fee)
  • Coupon code field present at both cart and checkout ("YALLA40" confirmed live); one coupon per order, excluded on Flash Deal items
  • Multi-vendor orders itemize shipping per seller (e.g. "Delivered by Comms and Goods LLC — AED 1.00")
  • Delivery estimate shown contextually (e.g. "24–48 hours" for Dubai)

Account Area

CUST-05
confirmed
  • Profile Settings — name, phone, saved addresses (multiple, typed), change password
  • Order History — status-tabbed (All / Pending / Processing / Shipped / Delivered / Cancelled)
  • Returns — dedicated list view tied to completed orders
  • My Reviews — gated to "view orders to write reviews" (purchase-gated review submission)

Auth

CUST-06
confirmed
  • Google OAuth + email/password, with mandatory email verification step before first login
  • Server-side weak/breached-password rejection on signup
  • Backend runs on Lovable Cloud (Supabase-based), identifiable from the auth verification email's sender domain

Localization

CUST-07
deferred — not in this scope/cost
  • No language switcher on the live platform — English only, currently
  • Arabic/English localization is in progress on the web platform but not live yet, so there's no live reference to build against — out of this scope and cost rather than assumed
  • Product page layout (text containers, icon direction, spacing) is built RTL-aware from the start, so localization can switch on later at low cost instead of a layout retrofit
  • Full localization (translated content pipeline, RTL across every screen, not just product pages) comes into scope once the web platform has it live

B — Seller App

Seller Onboarding (KYC)

SELL-01
confirmed
  • Separate registration system from customer signup, at a dedicated /seller/register wizard with its own login
  • 4 steps: Account (name/email/password/phone or Google OAuth) → Business (store name, UAE business address, description) → Categories (multi-select from the full 19-category taxonomy) → Documents
  • Documents step is a hard gate: mandatory upload of UAE Trade License + Emirates ID (PDF/JPG/PNG, max 10MB), plus required Seller Agreement acceptance — submission is disabled without a file
  • Applications go into a review queue with email notification on approval, typically 2-3 business days

Seller Dashboard — Analytics

SELL-02
confirmed
  • Date-range filters (Today / 7 Days / 30 Days / 90 Days / All Time / Custom)
  • 8 KPI tiles: Total Sales, Total Orders, Pending Orders, Payouts Received, Returns, Low Stock Alerts, Pending Reviews, Cancelled Orders
  • Sales Trend chart, Orders by Status chart, Top Products by Revenue, Commission by Category
  • Recent Orders table and Low Stock Alerts panel, each with a "view all / manage" link into the full module

Product Management

SELL-03
confirmed — bigger than originally scoped
  • Product list with search (title/short name/SKU), a "Missing images" filter, and Bulk Upload
  • Add Product form is genuinely complex: Physical vs. Digital product type toggle with different field sets (digital = no shipping/stock, card-only payment, buyer gets an access link)
  • Title, slug, model number, SKU/barcode, description, price, original price, stock
  • Brand selector — searchable dropdown across 300+ existing brands
  • Category + subcategory cascade (same 19-category taxonomy used everywhere else)
  • Mandatory shipping dimensions (weight, length, width, height) — carrier-relevant, not decorative
  • Image upload with auto-resize and WebP conversion (spec: 1000×1000px recommended, under 2MB, auto-converted to ~300KB)
  • Freeform Specifications (add arbitrary key-value rows)
  • Trust Badges multi-select (Warranty Available, Product Assembling Available, Secure Payment, Upgrade Available, Condition: Brand new, Condition: Renewed) — these map directly to the "Sold by" box badges on the customer-facing product page
  • Product Variants (size/color, independent stock and price per variant)

Inventory

SELL-04
confirmed — not previously scoped
  • Separate nav item and separate screen from Products — a dedicated stock-management view beyond the per-product edit form

Orders & Returns

SELL-05
confirmed
  • Orders: status-filterable list view
  • Returns: dedicated list view with status tabs, mirroring the customer-side Returns module
  • Reviews: seller-facing view of customer reviews on their own products

Seller-Created Coupons

SELL-06
confirmed — not previously scoped
  • This is separate from the platform-wide coupon codes (like YALLA40) already scoped under CUST-04 — sellers can build and manage their own
  • KPIs: Total Coupons, Total Redemptions, Total Discount Given
  • Two views: "My Coupons" and a "Usage Report"

Seller-Created Flash Deals

SELL-07
confirmed — not previously scoped
  • Sellers can create their own flash deals, separate from the platform-curated homepage countdown deals on the customer side

Payouts

SELL-08
confirmed
  • Three states: Total Earned, Pending Payout, and Frozen (the last one likely ties to open disputes or return holds)
  • Requires IBAN/bank details in Settings before payouts activate
  • Payout History table; bi-weekly cadence

Settings & Onboarding Checklist

SELL-09
confirmed — not previously scoped
  • First login shows a 4-item checklist: Store information (name & phone), Warehouse/pickup address, Bank details for payouts, KYC documents on file
  • Warehouse/pickup address is a distinct required field — separate from the business address collected during registration

Buyer-Seller Messaging

CUST-08 / SELL-10
confirmed in scope — no live reference
  • In scope, though not present on the live web platform — built new rather than ported from an existing UI
  • Planned: conversation list (tied to an order or product), thread view, send/receive, read/unread state
  • Real-time delivery via WebSockets, matching the platform's existing messaging protocol — plus a push notification tie-in for new messages (shares infrastructure with A8 Notifications)
Admin — out of scope for now — Admin will be documented and costed the same way as everything above, once its scope is available to review.
