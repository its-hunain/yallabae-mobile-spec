Phased Delivery

Customer and Seller ship first, getting real users onto the platform as early as possible. Admin follows as its own phase once the app is live — same total scope, split into two natural stages.

Phase 1

Customer + Seller

Gets the app live — customers can shop, sellers can operate
99 working days
PKR 594,000
  • Full customer shopping experience — catalog, checkout, account, messaging
  • Full seller operations — products, orders, payouts, analytics, coupons
  • Independently launchable — the platform runs end-to-end on this alone
Phase 2

Admin

Brings platform-wide oversight into the mobile app
68 working days
PKR 408,000
  • Full admin panel — sellers, products, orders, payouts, merchandising, site config
  • Built on the same permission layer already in the Seller app — no rework needed to add it
  • Can start as soon as Phase 1 is delivered, or run in parallel with a second resource

Why split it this way: Customer and Seller are what make the platform usable — once Phase 1 ships, the business is running on mobile. Admin is internal tooling (used by one team, not published for a mass audience), so it doesn't block launch. Splitting the two means a smaller first commitment and an earlier live date, without changing the total scope or cost.
Phase 2 can start immediately after Phase 1, or run in parallel on a second resource if the timeline needs to compress — see the app structure note for how Seller and Admin share one combined app and permission layer.
